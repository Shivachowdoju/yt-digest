Privacy Policy – yt-daily-digest

yt-daily-digest is a personal tool used only by its owner. It requests read-only access to the owner's YouTube subscriptions to list newly published videos. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Video titles and public transcripts are sent to the Anthropic API solely to generate the owner's personal study notes. OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
